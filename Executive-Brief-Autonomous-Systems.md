# Executive Brief — Autonomous Systems

Autonomous systems require more than perception and prediction.
They require an awareness‑driven layer capable of interpreting crisis evolution.

This brief outlines the structural weaknesses in current autonomous stacks:

1. **Ambiguity Collapse**  
   ML‑based systems reduce multi‑state conditions into a single output,
   eliminating critical crisis information.

2. **Unstable Decision Pathways**  
   When environmental data shifts rapidly, decision pathways lose coherence
   and become vulnerable to cascading errors.

3. **Lack of Crisis Evolution Modeling**  
   Autonomous systems treat crises as isolated events rather than evolving states
   with internal structure and temporal dependencies.

4. **Absence of Awareness Architecture**  
   Without a layer that interprets ambiguity, entanglement, and multi‑state dynamics,
   autonomous systems cannot stabilize decisions in complex environments.

A next‑generation autonomous decision architecture must:

• hold multiple states in superposition  
• maintain entangled decision pathways  
• stabilize ambiguity before it becomes action  
• interpret crisis evolution instead of raw sensor data  

This document is intended for executive‑level review in autonomous R&D.
