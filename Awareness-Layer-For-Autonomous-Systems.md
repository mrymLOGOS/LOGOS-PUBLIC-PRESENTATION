# Awareness Layer for Autonomous Systems

Autonomous systems require more than perception and prediction.
They require a stabilizing layer that interprets ambiguity and crisis evolution.

An awareness layer provides:
• multi‑state superposition  
• entangled decision coherence  
• clarity‑driven stabilization  
• resilience against contradictory data  

This layer does not replace existing autonomous stacks.
It enhances them by preserving the structure of crisis states
and preventing decision collapse in ambiguous environments.
