# Autonomous Research Notes

Key observations in autonomous crisis behavior:

• Crisis states evolve across multiple layers of perception  
• Ambiguity is structural, not accidental  
• Decision collapse occurs when multi‑state conditions are compressed  
• Entangled pathways maintain coherence under uncertainty  
• Awareness‑centric architectures reduce instability in ambiguous environments  

These notes summarize foundational concepts for next‑generation autonomous R&D.
